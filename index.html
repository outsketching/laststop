LAST STOP

The city doesn't stop for you. Hold to stop walking. Release to move. At tram crossings, stop behind the line, check the signal and both directions, then cross safely. Move your cursor left and right to look along the tracks. On touch screens, hold to brake and slide your finger to look. While walking, spot lit windows, rooftop birds and street posters: glance towards the outlined detail before it fades to build a spotting chain. On mobile, use the LOOK buttons to look while walking. Aim for the white STOP band before each crossing. The surrounding bands fade to grey as landing quality drops. Stops are graded PERFECT, GREAT, GOOD, OKAY or MISS by position. Braking is heavier: anticipate the longer slide. PERFECT and GREAT stops launch quickly; OKAY and MISS stops take much longer to rebuild momentum. Crossings arrive more often. Spotting pauses near crossings; always wait for the signal.
